Prodsee is a cloud production planner for manufacturing companies. It keeps your products, BOMs, customer orders, production orders and shipments in one place and shows what is being made, from what, and for when.

It starts where spreadsheets stop being enough. You begin with the orders on your desk today: no consultants, no six-month rollout.

## What works today

- **BOM catalog.** Multi-level BOM trees that you build and edit in seconds. Products and materials, as a tree or as a table.
- **Just-In-Time BOM™.** You do not have to build the whole product catalog before you start. Create products and materials when an order needs them, and the catalog grows as you work.
- **Customer orders.** Write down what the customer ordered, how many and for when. The status sets itself, and every line shows how much is already in production orders and how much has shipped.
- **Production orders and planning.** Create production orders and lay them out in time on the order board: one clear picture of what is made when, and when it will be ready.
- **Work progress.** The whole production at a glance: what is in progress, what is overdue, what is done. Report finished quantities, and Pamięć hali™ (shop-floor memory) remembers the path each part takes, with no routings to configure.
- **Shipments.** One shipment is one transport: a truck, a container, a courier. Take lines from several orders or ship one order in parts, and Prodsee counts what is left to send.
- **Reports.** Download what the shop floor reported and what the planned orders will need as an Excel sheet or a CSV file.
- **Import from Autodesk Inventor.** Export the bill of materials to Excel and point Prodsee at the file. You see everything in a preview before it reaches the catalog, and parts that are already there are recognized by their number.

## Who it is for

Large MES systems are priced, sized and designed for corporations. Spreadsheets and paper stop being enough as a company grows. Prodsee sits exactly in between, and bets on a quick start and value from day one.

- **Type of production:** discrete manufacturing, where products are made from countable parts according to a bill of materials and assembly.
- **Example industries:** metalworking and steel structures, furniture, machines and equipment, windows and doors, vehicle bodies, control cabinets and the like.
- **How you start:** from the orders you have today. You roll it out yourself, without an integration project.
- **How you pay:** one price per company, not per user.
